SpeedTest Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Last updated: November 8, 2026

SpeedTest ("the app", "we", "us") is an internet speed test app developed by Hoa Nguyen. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Summary
  • No account is required.
  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Feedback you submit is sent to GitHub and stored as a GitHub issue.
  • Speed tests and IP lookup requests are sent to third-party services.

Information you provide

Feedback and bug reports

When you submit the feedback form, the app sends:

  • the feedback type (Feedback, Feature Request, or Bug Report)
  • your message
  • your email address, if you choose to provide it
  • the app version, operating system and version, locale, and submission time

This information is sent over HTTPS to GitHub, Inc. and stored as an issue in the SpeedTest project repository. If the repository is public, the issue — and any email address you include — is publicly visible. Do not include passwords, tokens, or other sensitive information in feedback.

We use this information to understand and respond to feedback, reproduce bugs, and improve the app.

Speed tests

When you start a speed test, the app contacts Cloudflare's public speed test service at speed.cloudflare.com directly from your device. Cloudflare receives your IP address and network request metadata, as with any network connection. Download and upload measurements use generated data sent to and from your device; the app does not send your test results to us. Latency and jitter are calculated on your device.

Network information

When you open the Network tab, the app requests your public IP information from:

  • ipwho.is — https://ipwho.is
  • ipify — https://www.ipify.org

These providers receive your IP address and network request metadata and return information such as your public IP, ISP, organization, country, region, city, and timezone. Their own privacy policies apply. The app does not send these results to us.

Local network interfaces

The app reads local network interface names and addresses on your device to display them. This information stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Speed test results, history, and app preferences (theme and language) are kept in memory on your device for the current session. They are not transmitted to us. They may be cleared when you clear history or close the app.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — public speed test endpoints. https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • GitHub, Inc. — stores feedback issues. https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement
  • ipwho.is — IP geolocation lookup. https://ipwho.is
  • ipify — public IP lookup. https://www.ipify.org
  • Google Play — app distribution. https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Feedback is shared with GitHub as described above. Speed test and IP lookup providers process your request as described above.

Security

All network requests made by the app use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not include sensitive information in feedback.

Retention and deletion

Feedback issues are retained until they are resolved or deleted. You can request deletion of a feedback issue, including any email address you included, by contacting us at hoa.nguyenminh.it@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify the issue. GitHub may retain copies according to its own policies.

Local data is cleared when you clear history, close the app, or uninstall it.

Children

SpeedTest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Feedback is processed by GitHub in the United States. By submitting feedback, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where GitHub operates.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will be posted at this URL, with the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in an app update or a notice.
